Research

Two lines so far: drug crystallisation and light–matter interactions.

Pharmaceutical crystallisation

2025 – 2026

Jun – Aug 2026, Imperial College London

L-arginine as a soft template for vancomycin crystallisation

I tested whether L-arginine could steer how the antibiotic vancomycin crystallises, and how buffer concentration changes crystal formation and morphology.

  • Vapour diffusion
  • Soft templating
  • Opentrons OT-2

Jun – Aug 2025, Colgate University

Crystallising mandelic acid enantiomers

I grew crystals of each enantiomer by hot filtration for SFG-CD measurements, where my two research lines meet. Telling enantiomers apart matters for many chiral molecules, including pharmaceuticals.

Light–matter interactions

2024 – present

Oct 2024 – Present, Tecnológico de Monterrey and Colgate University

First steps toward the characterization of enantiomers with SFG-CD

Enantiomers are nearly identical, so they're hard to tell apart. I'm combining Second Harmonic and Sum Frequency Generation to see whether mandelic acid enantiomers respond differently to circularly polarized light.
